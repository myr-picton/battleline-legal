Delete your account and game data

Use the in-app deletion option when possible. If you cannot access the app, request deletion by email.

Battle Line처리 목표Target response: 7 days

방법 1. 앱에서 삭제

  1. Battle Line을 엽니다.
  2. 메뉴에서 설정을 선택합니다.
  3. 계정 삭제를 선택합니다.
  4. 삭제 안내를 읽고 다시 계정 삭제를 눌러 확인합니다.

서버가 요청을 확인하면 계정과 운영 데이터가 삭제되고 앱의 로컬 계정 정보도 제거됩니다. 처리 결과를 받기 전에 앱을 강제 종료하지 마세요.

앱에 접근할 수 없다면 아래 내용을 myr.picton@gmail.com으로 보내주세요. 계정 보호를 위해 추가 확인을 요청할 수 있습니다.

처리 기간

앱 내 삭제는 서버가 온라인이고 요청이 검증되면 즉시 처리됩니다. 이메일 요청에는 보통 7일 이내 응답하며, 추가 본인 확인이나 법적 사유가 있으면 최대 30일이 걸릴 수 있습니다.

Method 1. Delete in the app

  1. Open Battle Line.
  2. Select Settings from the menu.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Read the notice and select Delete account again to confirm.

After the server confirms the request, the account and live service data are deleted and local account data is removed. Do not force-close the app before the result arrives.

Method 2. Request by email

If you cannot access the app, send the following to myr.picton@gmail.com. We may request additional verification to protect the account.

Subject: Battle Line account deletion request Nickname: Country in the game: Google Play Games display name (if linked): Approximate date last played: Request: Please delete my Battle Line account and associated game data.

Do not send authentication tokens, passwords, OAuth client secrets, or service-account files.

Information deleted

  • Player account, authentication-token hash, nickname, country, and Google link
  • XP, rank, match records, items, and gems
  • Sync events, reports, blocks, and account links on reward claims
  • Local account and progress data when deletion is performed in the app

Limited information retained

  • Ad transaction and claim IDs may remain without player identifiers to prevent duplicate rewards and fraud.
  • Rotating backups created before deletion are not used for the live service and expire within no more than 14 days.
  • Information strictly needed for law, security investigations, or disputes may be retained only for that purpose and period.

Timing

In-app deletion is immediate after the online server validates the request. We normally respond to email requests within 7 days. Additional identity verification or legal requirements may extend completion up to 30 days.

Contact

Questions about deletion status: myr.picton@gmail.com
